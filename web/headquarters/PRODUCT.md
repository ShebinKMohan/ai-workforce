# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers coordinating a local AI workforce through runtimes already installed on their computer. The connected Headquarters application is `src/headquarters/index.html` and is served by the AI Employee coordinator in this same repository.

## Product Purpose

Control Center accepts a task, selects an appropriate department, and coordinates its head and specialists. The user can follow ownership, status, runtime events, independently verified outcomes, and concrete blockers. Headquarters is a canvas-first office for exploring departments and teammates. The right sidebar is a task feed by default, with conversations and traces accessible from individual cards.

## Personality

Clear, capable, approachable, and familiar to a developer working in VS Code or Cursor. Use a spacious interactive office canvas, crisp contrast, compact controls, a readable hierarchy, and 2D illustrated human avatars. Avoid stacked dashboard headings and nested organization cards. The user's latest screenshots and instructions override older prototype conventions.

## Product Principles

- Use the locally installed runtime's actual model and effort catalog. Keep configured execution identity and historical provenance truthful.
- Preserve department and agent management, project selection, attachments, drafts, and conversation context.
- New tasks are independent; followups in an opened task inherit its context. New messages do not cancel older work.
- Resolve routine issues through the orchestrator hierarchy and bounded internal repairs. Surface a real blocker when execution cannot continue.
- Show task status from durable coordinator facts. Never use simulated activity, made-up percentages, or business metrics without a source.
- Prefer one updated local Codex entry in this user's harness; keep the desktop application itself untouched.

## Accessibility

Keyboard-operable navigation and breadcrumbs, labeled status indicators, visible focus, independent scrolling, mobile adaptation, clear text/control contrast, and system reduced-motion support.
